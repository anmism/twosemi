Your whole workflow, one keystroke away

TwoSemi is a keyboard-first utility that lives in your system tray. Notes, reminders, todos, streaks, and AI chat from a single launcher that appears and disappears with a double-tap.

Download now

Notes

Capture text from whatever you're doing or type something new. Secrets are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never leave your device.

Reminders

Set a time, get a system notification. Snooze or complete from the launcher. No account, no sync, no server.

Todos

Group tasks by project. Toggle completion with a keystroke. Simple enough to not get in the way of the actual work.

Streaks

Track daily or weekday habits. Check in from the launcher. See your current run and best run at a glance.

AI Chat

Saved conversations with Gemini. Bring your own API key, pick a model, threads persist locally between sessions.

Local First

Everything stays on your machine in a single SQLite database. No telemetry, no accounts, no cloud.
